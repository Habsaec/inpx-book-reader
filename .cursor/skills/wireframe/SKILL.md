# Wireframe: Explore Many Ideas Quickly

Produce low-fidelity wireframes before committing to hi-fi. **Wireframes are disposable** — breadth of options, not fidelity of any one.

## Cursor notes

- For React projects: sketch as simple JSX with greyscale boxes and labels, or describe layouts in a markdown file with ASCII/wireframe blocks.
- Don't polish wireframes — they're throwaway exploration.

## Phase 1: Understand the goal

Confirm: what's being explored; user goal; constraints (mobile/desktop); **3+ variations** minimum; axis of variation (layout, density, step count, CTA placement).

## Phase 2: Wireframe conventions

- **Greyscale only** — black, white, 2–3 grays
- **System sans-serif** — no type personality
- **Labeled boxes** for content areas
- **Striped placeholders** for imagery — never real images
- **Skeleton copy** — no final copy
- **Annotations** on key decisions

## Phase 3: Sketch variations

At least 3 variations on the established axis. Vary layout, density, flow structure, CTA placement, navigation. Order from by-the-book to novel; include one off-distribution option.

## Phase 4: Annotate

2–4 annotation points per variation next to it:

```
- Variation 1 (wizard): simple, focused, but slow
- Variation 2 (single page): fastest path, heavy first impression
- Variation 3 (progressive): balances both, more state
```

## Phase 5: Hand off

Capture chosen variation, what attracted the user, what was rejected. Suggest hi-fi next step (`make-a-prototype` or direct implementation). Don't invest hi-fi polish in wireframes.
